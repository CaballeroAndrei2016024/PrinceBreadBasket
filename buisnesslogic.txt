Transactions:
-Both Admin and Client forms let users choose Beginning Inventory, Additions, or Sales. 
They can then select products and enter quantities.
-The  server looks up a product's name and price and recalculates the total and validate the transaction.
-This number is from the shared atomic counter in transaction-number.ts.
-Sales Transactions are only for Sales Headers, btw.
-Changes to Sales Transactions (for Sales Headers) as well as Beginning Inventory and Additions Transactions (for Sales Logs) will be reflected as well.
Reports:
-Reports comes from the Sales Header as snapshots, and do not store Sales Header Id.
Logs:
-Sales Logs fetch Transactions within the selected date range and group rows by product.
-The same logic of: 
1. Beginning Inventory, 
2.Additions, 
3.Sales (having the rows of Beginning Inventory and Additions combined), 
4.Total Available for Sale (uneditable section that adds the quantities and totals from Beginning Inventory and Additions), and 
5.Ending Inventory (uneditable section that calculates the portion of Total Available for Sales that isn't in the Sales.)
-Transaction Rows fetch the unit price directly from the Products Database.
Sales:
-Uneditable as Admin, Sales Headers come from Sales Transactions as a screenshot.
